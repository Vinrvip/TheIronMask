BEFORE THE MASK. THERE WAS BYTE FORCE.

OUR ROOTS
ARE IN VEX.

The experience we bring to FTC was built one VEX challenge at a time.

THIS IS WHERE
WE LEARNED TO BUILD.

Our journey began in VEX V5RC in 2024. In 2025, we became Byte Force. We designed, built, tested, and kept improving. We earned awards along the way—and learned the value of hard work, teamwork, and persistence.

These are the robots and mechanisms from that chapter. The experience behind them now comes with us to The Iron Mask: how to turn an idea into a mechanism, work through a difficult build, and learn from every test.
